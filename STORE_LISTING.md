### Full Description (Max 4,000 characters)

AptiRise is an intuitive, offline-friendly educational app designed to help students and job seekers master quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, and verbal ability. Whether preparing for campus placements, competitive recruitment exams, or skill assessments, AptiRise offers structured learning tools to track your progress and boost your confidence.

KEY FEATURES:

• 10 CORE APTITUDE TOPICS: Practice curated questions across Percentages, Ratios, Averages, Profit & Loss, Time & Work, Number Series, Direction Sense, Syllogisms, Grammar & Vocabulary, and Reading Comprehension.

• STARTER QUESTION BANK: Access 60 original, checked practice questions with clear step-by-step explanations and helpful problem-solving hints.

• FULL-LENGTH TIMED MOCK TESTS: Test your exam readiness under timed conditions with 3 starter mock tests. Enjoy process-death recovery so your timer and answers remain safe even if your app is interrupted.

• STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATIONS & BOOKMARKS: Review detailed answer explanations after every practice session or test. Save challenging questions to your personal Bookmarks list for quick revision anytime.

• FORMULA & CONCEPT LIBRARY: Access key mathematical formulas, shortcuts, and core concepts directly within each topic section.

• REAL PROGRESS DASHBOARD: Track your performance with real local statistics, including overall accuracy percentage, total completed attempts, and 7-day activity insights.

• OPTIONAL CLOUD BACKUP & SYNC: Optionally back up your verified learning records to the cloud to keep your data safe across devices. Works completely offline when disabled.

• PRIVACY & TRANSPARENT CONTROL: Complete control over your account with one-tap account and cloud data deletion directly inside the app.

Download AptiRise today and build your aptitude skills step by step!
