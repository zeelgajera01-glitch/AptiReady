AptiRise – Account Deletion Request

This page allows AptiRise users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email from the email address associated with your AptiRise account.
  2. Use the subject: AptiRise Account Deletion Request
  3. Include your AptiRise account email address in the message.
Request Account Deletion

What will be deleted

We will delete your AptiRise account and associated account data, subject to information that we are legally required to retain.

This may include account identifiers and data associated with your AptiRise account.
