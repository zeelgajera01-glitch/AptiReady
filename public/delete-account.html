AptiRise Local Data & Earlier Accounts

Updated 18 October 2026.

AptiRise works without an account. The current version does not request a name, email address or password, and includes no login, profile, advertising or cloud synchronization functionality.

Learning records

Practice attempts, answers, mock test scores, bookmarks, hint allowance and appearance preferences are stored on your device. They are not uploaded or restored across devices.

Update checks

The app can contact Firebase Remote Config to check the minimum supported version. Learning records are not sent with this check.

Removing local data

Clear AptiRise storage in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove local records. This cannot be undone.

Earlier accounts and support

If you used an older version with an account, contact zeelgajera010@gmail.com for help with those earlier records or a deletion request.
